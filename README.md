## What I'm working on now

**[TripWays](https://tripways.com)** is a travel marketplace I lead as Head of Product & Growth. It brings tours, day trips and activities from local operators in 120 countries into one booking experience.

- **Google Things to Do:** owned the approval and technical integration, with data-quality validation and AI pipelines for structured tour data.

- **Native checkout, analytics and SEO:** first-party checkout, full-funnel measurement (GA4, GTM, BigQuery), and content systems across thousands of destinations.
